**Author:** Lead Scientific Communications & Academic Outreach Agent

## Abstract
In computational ecosystems and thermodynamics-based simulation frameworks such as the *Web of Life*, maintaining strict mass conservation and quantifying disequilibrium are foundational for simulating self-organizing biogeochemical cycles (Carbon, Nitrogen, Phosphorus, and Water). Sprint 072 introduces a specialized pure helper function, `computeAbsoluteStockDelta(actual, expected)`, designed to calculate element-wise absolute discrepancies between observed thermodynamic state vectors (`actual`) and baseline or predicted vectors (`expected`). By operating within a pure functional paradigm, this module guarantees deterministic, side-effect-free evaluations that feed regulatory monad feedback loops. These loops drive systemic equilibration toward maximum entropy production limits bounded by external solar forcing, while rigorously enforcing the First and Second Laws of Thermodynamics.
