## Abstract
Living systems and complex ecological structures maintain their highly ordered configurations far from thermodynamic equilibrium by continuously dissipating free energy and exporting entropy. This preprint details Sprint 001 of the *Web of Life* simulation engine, which establishes the foundational thermodynamic contract: **RFC 001**. We introduce strict TypeScript interface contracts for energetic, entropic, and exergic metrics alongside the abstract base class `ThermodynamicStructure`. This architecture enforces mass-energy conservation (First Law) and unidirectional internal entropy generation ($\dot{S}_{\text{gen}} \ge 0$, Second Law), coupled with exergy destruction tracking via the Gouy-Stodola theorem.
