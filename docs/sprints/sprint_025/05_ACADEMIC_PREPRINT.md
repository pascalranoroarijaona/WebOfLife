## Abstract
In computational systems ecology and planetary-scale biosphere simulation, spatial discretization dictates the granularity of biogeochemical cycling, energy flux distribution, and trophic network interactions. Sprint 025 establishes the formal resolution tier boundary check function for Uber's H3 hierarchical hexagonal spatial index within the *Web of Life* engine. Restricting spatial resolution tiers strictly to the integer domain $r \in [0, 15]$ prevents topological corruption and maintains rigorous thermodynamic compliance. This paper outlines the theoretical framework governing spatial refinement, mass conservation (First Law of Thermodynamics), and solar energy flux allocation (Second Law of Thermodynamics) under monadic state transitions.
