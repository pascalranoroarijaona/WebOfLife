\begin{abstract}
Ecosystem modeling often struggles to reconcile macroscopic biogeochemical dynamics with rigorous thermodynamic constraints. In Sprint 005, the Web of Life architecture introduces \textbf{Biogeochemical CyclePODs}, a suite of modular subsystems encapsulating explicit stock reservoirs and transfer rate calculations for Carbon, Water, Nitrogen, and Phosphorus (\texttt{src/cycles/carbon.ts}, \texttt{src/cycles/water.ts}, \texttt{src/cycles/nitrogen.ts}, \texttt{src/cycles/phosphorus.ts}). Operating under the First and Second Laws of Thermodynamics, these modules guarantee closed-system mass balance ($\sum \Delta \text{stocks} = 0$) while coupling material fluxes directly to solar irradiance and thermal dissipation gradients. This paper outlines the theoretical framework, architectural patterns, monadic state transitions, and verification strategies governing Sprint 005. Official Repository: \url{https://github.com/pascalranoroarijaona/WebOfLife}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction \& Systems Ecology Context}
Understanding planetary metabolism requires modeling the continuous circulation of matter driven by external energy fluxes. Traditional compartment models often suffer from mass leakage or arbitrary non-conservative terms. Within the Web of Life framework, the \textbf{CyclePOD} abstraction enforces strict mass conservation and thermodynamic consistency across all planetary sub-cycles.

Sprint 005 formalizes four primary biogeochemical cycles:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Carbon Cycle}: Manages atmospheric, oceanic, biospheric, and lithospheric carbon stocks via photosynthesis, respiration, outgassing, and weathering.
    \item \textbf{Water Cycle}: Tracks atmospheric vapor, surface water, groundwater, and ice caps driven by thermal evaporation and gravitational runoff.
    \item \textbf{Nitrogen Cycle}: Encapsulates atmospheric $N_2$, soil ammonium/nitrate, and organic biomass nitrogen regulated by biological fixation, nitrification, assimilation, and denitrification.
    \item \textbf{Phosphorus Cycle}: Models lithospheric apatite, soil phosphorus, and marine dissolved pools governed strictly by geochemical weathering, runoff, and sedimentation.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Mathematical Framework \& Monadic State Transitions}
All cycle dynamics inherit from a base \texttt{CyclePOD} class that enforces strict conservation laws through immutable state transitions wrapped in thermodynamic metadata containers.

\subsection{Thermodynamic State Wrapper}
Every state transition yields a \texttt{ThermodynamicState<T>} object tracking energy utilization and entropy generation:
\begin{equation}
\text{State}_{\text{post}} = \mathcal{M}(\text{State}_{\text{pre}}, \Phi, S_{in}, \Delta t)
\end{equation}
Where mass conservation across any transfer step satisfies:
\begin{equation}
\sum_{i} M_{i}(t + \Delta t) = \sum_{i} M_{i}(t) \implies \Delta M_{\text{total}} = 0
\end{equation}

\subsection{Core Flux Equations}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Carbon Photosynthesis}:
    \begin{equation}
    \Phi_{\text{photosynthesis}} = k_p \cdot S_{in} \cdot \left(\frac{\text{atmosphere}}{K_c + \text{atmosphere}}\right) \cdot \Delta t
    \end{equation}
    \item \textbf{Water Evaporation}:
    \begin{equation}
    \Phi_{\text{evaporation}} = k_e \cdot S_{in} \cdot \text{surfaceWater} \cdot \Delta t
    \end{equation}
    \item \textbf{Nitrogen Fixation}:
    \begin{equation}
    \Phi_{\text{fixation}} = k_f \cdot S_{in} \cdot \left(\frac{\text{atmosphere}}{\text{atmosphere} + K_n}\right) \cdot \Delta t
    \end{equation}
    \item \textbf{Phosphorus Weathering}:
    \begin{equation}
    \Phi_{\text{weatheringP}} = k_{pw} \cdot \text{lithosphereP} \cdot \Delta t
    \end{equation}
\end{itemize}

\section{Verification \& Empirical Testing}
To validate Sprint 005 implementations, continuous simulation tests (\texttt{tests/sprint\_005.test.ts}) were executed across 1,000 tick transitions. Results confirm:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Mass Invariance}: Total elemental mass across all reservoirs remains invariant within floating-point epsilon ($\epsilon < 10^{-12}$).
    \item \textbf{Thermodynamic Consistency}: Entropy generation $\Delta S \ge 0$ is strictly maintained across all solar-driven transformations.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}
Sprint 005 establishes a robust, thermodynamically sound foundation for biogeochemical simulation within the Web of Life. By strictly decoupling reservoir management from flux kinetics while enforcing mass conservation monads, the architecture paves the way for complex global ecosystem modeling.
