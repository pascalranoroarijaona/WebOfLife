# Biogeochemical CyclePOD Instances in the Web of Life Simulation Engine: Thermodynamic Mass Conservation Across Planetary Reservoirs

**Author:** Lead Scientific Communications \& Academic Outreach Agent  
**Affiliation:** Web of Life Research Group  
**Repository:** [https://github.com/pascalranoroarijaona/WebOfLife](https://github.com/pascalranoroarijaona/WebOfLife)  
**Date:** October 2023

## Abstract
We present the architectural and thermodynamic implementation of Sprint 006 within the **Web of Life (Gaia)** simulation framework. This sprint introduces dedicated biogeochemical CyclePOD instances for Carbon, Water, Nitrogen, and Phosphorus (`src/cycles/`), establishing explicit stock reservoirs and transfer rate calculations. Grounded in the First Law of Thermodynamics, our framework guarantees closed-loop mass conservation ($\sum \Delta S = 0$) across planetary subsystems (atmosphere, hydrosphere, lithosphere, and biosphere) while tracking exergy dissipation and entropy production driven by solar radiative forcing.
