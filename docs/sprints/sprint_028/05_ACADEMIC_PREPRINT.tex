\begin{abstract}
Ecosystem simulation frameworks frequently struggle to reconcile localized metabolic activity with global conservation laws. In this preprint, we summarize the architectural advancements introduced in \textbf{Sprint 028} of the Web of Life project. We formalize a rigorous thermodynamic ledger framework that enforces the First Law of Thermodynamics (mass-energy conservation) across spatial graphs with sub-atomic precision ($\Delta M < 10^{-12}$) alongside the Second Law via explicit entropy generation ($\Delta S = Q / T$). Furthermore, we introduce spatial nutrient gradients (\texttt{SpatialNode}, \texttt{BiomePatch}) and expand our trophic hierarchy to include specialized detritivore monad stock transitions, ensuring zero matter loss during organismal senescence and scavenging.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and Theoretical Framework}
Traditional ecological modeling often treats nutrient pools and organismal biomass as loosely coupled compartments, frequently suffering from numerical drift or unphysical mass creation/destruction. Within the Web of Life paradigm, organisms and environmental patches are unified under a rigorous thermodynamic and mass-balanced monad architecture.

Sprint 028 bridges spatial resource distribution, organismal movement costs, and multi-tier trophic cascades. The central objectives are:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Strict Mass Conservation:} Guaranteeing that atomic stocks ($C, N, P, H_2O$) are strictly conserved across all state transitions.
    \item \textbf{Exergy Dissipation \& Entropy Tracking:} Quantifying metabolic and kinetic heat loss to increment global entropy monotonically.
    \item \textbf{Spatial Equilibrium:} Implementing continuous diffusion-consumption nutrient patches (\texttt{BiomePatch}) that govern autotrophic uptake and heterotrophic foraging.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Core Mathematical Formalization}

\subsection{First and Second Laws of Thermodynamics}
For any closed system simulation graph, the total mass remains invariant:
\begin{equation}
\sum \text{Mass}_{\text{inputs}} = \sum \text{Mass}_{\text{outputs}} + \text{Mass}_{\text{excreted/resorbed}}
\end{equation}

Metabolic transformations incur obligatory heat dissipation ($Q$). The global entropy increment $\Delta S_{\text{total}}$ satisfies:
\begin{equation}
\Delta S_{\text{total}} = \Delta S_{\text{system}} + \frac{Q_{\text{dissipated}}}{T_{\text{ambient}}} \ge 0
\end{equation}

\subsection{Detritivore Scavenging \& Decomposition}
Upon organismal death, biomass is converted into a \texttt{Carcass} and deposited directly into the local \texttt{BiomePatch}. Detritivores process this carcass with an assimilation efficiency $\eta_{\text{detritivore}} \approx 0.15$:
\begin{equation}
\text{Assimilated} = \eta \cdot \text{Carcass}, \quad \text{Residue} = (1 - \eta) \cdot \text{Carcass}
\end{equation}
Oxidation of unassimilated carbon releases thermal energy $Q = C_{\text{carcass}} \times 10.5 \text{ J}$, directly updating the \texttt{ThermodynamicLedger}.

\section{System Architecture \& Implementation}

The implementation extends our core abstract classes without introducing parallel inheritance trees:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{\texttt{SpatialNode} / \texttt{BiomePatch}}: Manages localized carrying capacities and elemental stocks.
    \item \textbf{\texttt{ThermodynamicLedger}}: Audits mass conservation and tracks cumulative system entropy.
    \item \textbf{\texttt{DetritivoreMonad}}: Executes circular matter transitions, preventing ecological deadlocks.
\end{itemize}

\begin{lstlisting}
@dataclass
class ThermodynamicLedger:
    total_entropy: float = 0.0
    total_dissipated_heat: float = 0.0
    initial_system_mass: Optional[ElementalStocks] = None

    def record_dissipation(self, joules: float, ambient_temp: float = 298.15) -> None:
        if joules < 0:
            raise ValueError("Dissipated heat cannot be negative.")
        self.total_entropy += joules / ambient_temp
        self.total_dissipated_heat += joules

    def audit_mass_conservation(self, current_mass: ElementalStocks) -> float:
        if self.initial_system_mass is None:
            self.initial_system_mass = current_mass
            return 0.0
        return float(
            abs(current_mass.carbon - self.initial_system_mass.carbon) +
            abs(current_mass.nitrogen - self.initial_system_mass.nitrogen) +
            abs(current_mass.phosphorus - self.initial_system_mass.phosphorus) +
            abs(current_mass.water - self.initial_system_mass.water)
        )
\end{lstlisting}
