\begin{abstract}
Ecosystem simulations often suffer from numerical drift and mass-balance violations across complex biogeochemical cycles. Sprint 61 introduces the \textbf{Thermodynamic State Vector Inventory Discrepancy Evaluator} (\texttt{src/thermodynamics/state\_validator.ts}), a robust verification module designed to enforce First and Second Law thermodynamic constraints within the Web of Life ecosystem architecture. By systematically comparing actual stock state deltas against integrated flux-derived expectations across Carbon, Nitrogen, Phosphorus, and Water pools, the \texttt{StateValidator} computes absolute discrepancies and evaluates global mass conservation under strict floating-point tolerances ($\tau = 10^{-6}$). This preprint outlines the mathematical foundations, architectural design, and verification suite for Sprint 61.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and Systems Ecology Context}
In biophysical modeling, maintaining strict thermodynamic consistency is vital for long-term simulation stability. Ecosystems operate as open thermodynamic systems driven by external solar and radiative inputs, yet internal matter cycles (C, N, P, $H_2O$) must strictly obey mass conservation principles. 

Sprint 61 addresses the challenge of inventory discrepancies---divergences between observed stock variations ($\Delta S_{\text{actual}}$) and integrated boundary fluxes ($\Delta S_{\text{expected}}$)---by implementing the \texttt{StateValidator} component within the Web of Life framework.

\section{Thermodynamic \& Mass Balance Framework}

\subsection{First Law: Conservation of Mass}
For any stock pool $S_i$ over a discrete time step $\Delta t$, the actual stock delta is defined as:
\begin{equation}
\Delta S_{i, \text{actual}} = S_i(t + \Delta t) - S_i(t)
\end{equation}

The expected flux-derived delta is computed via integrated boundary fluxes (inflows minus outflows):
\begin{equation}
\Delta S_{i, \text{expected}} = \Delta t \cdot \sum_{j} J_{ji}
\end{equation}
where $J_{ji}$ represents net flux rates entering or leaving stock $i$ via pathway $j$.

\subsection{Discrepancy Metrics and Tolerance}
The absolute discrepancy $\epsilon_i$ for each stock key $i$ is formalized as:
\begin{equation}
\epsilon_i = \left| \Delta S_{i, \text{actual}} - \Delta S_{i, \text{expected}} \right|
\end{equation}

Total system absolute discrepancy $E_{\text{total}}$ is aggregated across all elemental pools:
\begin{equation}
E_{\text{total}} = \sum_{i} \epsilon_i
\end{equation}

Mass conservation is preserved if and only if:
\begin{equation}
E_{\text{total}} \le \tau_{\text{tolerance}}
\end{equation}
where default $\tau_{\text{tolerance}} = 10^{-6}$.

\section{Verification \& Test Suite Specifications}
The validation suite (\texttt{tests/sprint\_061.test.ts}) tests three primary operational modes:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Test 1 (Perfect Equilibrium / Conservation)}: Verifies zero discrepancy when actual deltas perfectly match flux-derived expectations across C, N, P, and $H_2O$ pools.
    \item \textbf{Test 2 (Unmodeled Perturbation Detection)}: Detects non-zero absolute discrepancies when unmodeled external forces perturb specific stock vectors.
    \item \textbf{Test 3 (Tolerance Boundary Thresholding)}: Confirms that the \texttt{isMassConserved} boolean correctly toggles based on whether total discrepancies breach $\tau = 10^{-6}$.
\end{enumerate}
