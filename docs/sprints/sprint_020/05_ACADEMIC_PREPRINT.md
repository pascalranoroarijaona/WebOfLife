## Abstract
As ecological and biophysical simulations scale across complex geographical grids, maintaining strict topological integrity is paramount for energy and matter routing within biosphere layers. Sprint 20 introduces a rigorous validation helper function, `validateH3Length`, designed to verify that spatial identifiers conform to Uber's 15-character hex string specification. Framed through the lens of nonequilibrium thermodynamics and systems ecology, this preprint outlines how pure logical operations preserve closed-loop system boundaries, ensuring zero net mass and energy dissipation ($\Delta M = 0, \Delta E = 0$) while bounding informational entropy.
