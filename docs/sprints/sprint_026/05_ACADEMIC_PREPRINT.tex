\begin{abstract}
Sprint 026 establishes the rigorous enforcement of thermodynamic laws across the Web of Life simulation engine. Moving beyond structural population dynamics, this sprint details class hierarchies, monad stock state transformations, and interface contracts required to model multi-trophic cascades, metabolic heat dissipation, and strict solar-only energy influx. We formalize energy conservation ($\Delta E_{sys} = 0$) and atomic mass balance (Carbon, Nitrogen pools) alongside Second Law entropy generation ($\Delta S_{gen} \ge 0$). Executable Python monad pipelines demonstrate deterministic state transitions across primary producers, consumers, and environmental detrital sinks.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and Thermodynamic Framework}
The \textbf{Web of Life} simulation models complex adaptive ecosystems as closed thermodynamic systems bounded by a single external exergy source: electromagnetic radiation flux from a `SolarSource` singleton. 

\subsection{First and Second Laws of Thermodynamics}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Energy Conservation (1st Law):} Total system energy ($E_{sys}$) is conserved across all transitions:
    $$\Delta E_{sys} = E_{solar\_in} - E_{dissipated} = 0$$
    Matter (carbon, nitrogen pools) must be strictly conserved; atoms cannot be created or destroyed.
    \item \textbf{Entropy Generation (2nd Law):} Every metabolic transaction, movement, and predation event incurs an entropy tax, releasing thermal energy ($Q$) into the environmental sink:
    $$\Delta S_{gen} = \frac{\dot{Q}_{dissipated}}{T_{env}} \ge 0$$
\end{enumerate}

\section{Class Hierarchy and Interface Contracts}
We extend the object-oriented architecture to support multi-trophic interactions and rigorous material/energy tracking:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Abiotic:} \texttt{SolarSource}, \texttt{Atmosphere}, \texttt{SoilMatrix}
    \item \textbf{Biotic:} \texttt{Organism} (Abstract Base) $\to$ \texttt{Autotroph} (Plants/Algae) \& \texttt{Heterotroph} (Herbivores, Carnivores, Detritivores).
\end{itemize}

Core interface contracts enforce strict typing:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \texttt{IThermodynamicSystem}: \texttt{get\_energy\_stock() $\to$ float}, \texttt{dissipate\_heat(joules: float) $\to$ None}
    \item \texttt{IMaterialPool}: \texttt{transfer\_mass(target, stoichiometry) $\to$ bool}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{State Transition Equations}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Autotroph Production:}
    $$E_{stored}(t+dt) = E_{stored}(t) + (\text{Solar Flux} \times \eta_{photo} - \text{Respiration}) \cdot dt$$
    \item \textbf{Heterotroph Trophic Transfer:}
    $$\Delta E_{ingested} = \epsilon_{assimilation} \cdot E_{prey\_consumed}$$
    $$\text{Heat Dissipated} = (1 - \epsilon_{assimilation}) \cdot E_{prey\_consumed} + \text{Basal Cost}$$
\end{enumerate}
